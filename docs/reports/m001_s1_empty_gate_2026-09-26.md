# M001 Lead #4 — S1 Empty-Gate Experiment

## Result

An S1-level singleton classifier improves holdoutB macro F0.5 from **0.971524** to **0.971670** (**+0.000146**). The gain is real but too small to justify a new submission by itself.

| System | Macro F0.5 | US | India | Singleton accuracy | Precision | Recall | Predictions / S1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| M001 baseline | 0.971524 | 0.972753 | 0.969657 | 0.947356 | 0.987850 | 0.944850 | 3.312474 |
| M001 + empty gate | **0.971670** | **0.972904** | **0.969796** | **0.958486** | **0.988039** | 0.944710 | 3.311349 |

The frozen threshold is 0.475. It suppresses all assignments for 12,732 of 220,319 holdoutB S1 records. Singleton accuracy rises by 0.01113 while recall falls by 0.00014. Both countries improve, which is a useful sanity check.

## Protocol

- Input is the existing `artifacts/scored_pairs.parquet`; the pair matcher is not retrained.
- The baseline decoder remains one-owner with `tau=0.6` and `delta=0.3`.
- The gate uses only inference-time aggregates of candidate scores, owned-pair scores, owner margins, and accepted-pair counts.
- Five-fold out-of-fold predictions on holdoutA choose the gate threshold.
- A final gate model is trained on all holdoutA S1 records and evaluated once on holdoutB.
- The reconstructed baseline is 0.971523862, matching the original M001 result.

The holdoutA optimum is broad but shallow: thresholds 0.475 through 0.575 score 0.970456–0.970461. The most important gate features are maximum accepted score, maximum owned score, accepted count, and maximum candidate score.

## Recommendation

Keep the gate as an optional final-stage component, but do not generate a new submission for this gain alone. It is most useful as a small additive improvement after a stronger matcher or France-focused feature experiment. Since France has no labels, test-time use should first compare the gate's suppression rate and score distribution by country; a material France shift would be a reason not to apply it there unchanged.
